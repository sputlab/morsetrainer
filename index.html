How it works

Morse is built from two signals: a dot (1 time unit) and a dash (3 time units).

Gaps: 1 unit between symbols of the same letter, 3 units between letters, 7 units between words.

Speed is measured in WPM (words per minute). At 15 WPM one unit lasts 80 ms — the exact duration of a dot.

In the Keyer tab, press and release quickly for a dot, hold for a dash. The GPU-accelerated timing bar shows the 1u and 3u thresholds in real time.
