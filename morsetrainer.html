Hold SPACE (or the pad on touch) briefly = dot, longer = dash. Pause to separate letters, pause longer to separate words.
How it works

Morse is built from two signals: a dot (1 time unit) and a dash (3 time units).

Gaps: 1 unit between symbols of the same letter, 3 units between letters, 7 units between words.

Speed is measured in WPM (words per minute). At 15 WPM one unit lasts about 80 ms — that's the duration of a dot.

In the Keyer tab, press and release quickly for a dot, hold for a dash. Watch the timing bar: the ideal dot ends at the first marker, the ideal dash at the second one.
